      Why it works

      • Recall checks. Testing yourself from memory beats rereading your notes. Roediger & Karpicke 2006; Dunlosky et al. 2013
      • Spaced review. Reviews spread over days beat the same time spent cramming. Cepeda et al. 2006
      • If-then plans. Deciding in advance what you'll do when distracted makes you likelier to follow through. Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006
      • Parking lot. Writing down a plan for a nagging task quiets it while you work. Masicampo & Baumeister 2011
      • Timed breaks. Students who took scheduled breaks felt less tired than those who broke off whenever. Biwer et al. 2023
      • Weekly review. Keeping track of progress toward a goal helps you reach it. Harkin et al. 2016
      • Sleep. Sleep is when the day's learning is consolidated into memory. Diekelmann & Born 2010

        Windows 10 / 11

        TaktOwl for Windows

        The owl lives next to the clock. Give Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp or any site or app a daily allowance. When it runs out, the owl closes the browser tab or the app. Works in Edge, Chrome, Firefox, Brave and Opera, with no extension needed.

        • One breath first. Opening a limited site or app brings up a 10-second breathing pause and the choice to go back. A pause like this cut app openings by about half in a field study (Grüning et al. 2023).
        • Commitment mode. Stricter limits start now, looser ones only tomorrow. Allcott et al. 2022
        • 10 minutes a platform. The default for social media. Hunt et al. 2018
        • Bedtime lock, and a weekly review every Monday.

        Phone

        Use your phone's own app timers

        Android and iPhone don't let any downloaded app close other apps. Their built-in timers do exactly that, and a student can't simply swipe them away.

        1. Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls → Dashboard → pick the app → App timer. When time's up, the app pauses until midnight.
        2. iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit → pick the apps → set the time. Turn on Block at End of Limit.
        3. At night: Android's Bedtime mode (Digital Wellbeing) and iPhone's Downtime (Screen Time) keep apps closed while you sleep.
